## PART 16 — STRICT RULES

1. Build in the existing repository; do not stop at a UI prototype or static mockup.
2. No hard-coded execution results; no animation disconnected from execution data.
3. Nothing from the out-of-scope list; no unnecessary dependencies; no over-engineering.
4. Keep the repository's conventions; TypeScript strict, no `any`.
5. Handle loading, error and empty states.

**Environment safety:**

- Do not commit, push or merge.
- Do not start, stop, restart or reconfigure an already running dev server, and do not
  change its port.
- Do not change environment variables unless unavoidable (explain why in the report).
- Do not delete unrelated files or working functionality.
