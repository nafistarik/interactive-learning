## PART 12 — DESIGN AND UX

A serious, premium developer-learning tool, not an LMS template.

- Clean, minimal, clear hierarchy, strong typography, consistent spacing.
- Design tokens (colour, spacing, radius, shadow) defined once and used everywhere.
- High-quality dark and light themes; follow the system theme until the user picks one.
- The Practice view gives the editor and visualization most of the screen.
- Controls are easy to find; useful loading, empty and error states everywhere.
- Keyboard accessible: focus visible, controls reachable by keyboard.
- The visualization is the visually rich part; the rest stays quiet.

Avoid: heavy gradients, glassmorphism, decorative animation, oversized headings,
card clutter, placeholder content, fake charts or fake results.
